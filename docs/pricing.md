# Packages and pricing (suggested; adjust to your market)

| Package | Monthly | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | PKR 15,000 | Up to 500 documents/month, Excel & CSV export, standard fields and line items, email support |
| **Business** | PKR 35,000 | Up to 2,000 documents/month, custom fields and templates, team access with roles, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | High volume, Google Sheets / accounting software integration, email inbox automation, private hosting |

- **One-time setup:** PKR 20,000-50,000 (branding, templates matched to their Excel, pilot tuning, training video).
- **Large volumes:** per-document price instead of a plan.
- **First 2-3 clients:** low-priced one-month pilot in exchange for a testimonial and permission to share measured numbers.
- **Running cost to you:** a small VPS or Render instance; offline mode has no per-document cost; Groq free tier covers light AI use.

Prices shown on the landing page live in `static/landing.html`; change them there.

## Add-ons to sell later
Email inbox automation (forward bills to an address), direct posting to accounting software, expense categories, approval workflows, mobile photo-capture app, more languages, custom reports.

## Freelance basics
- Agree scope, deliverables, timeline and price in writing before starting.
- Take an advance (e.g. 50%) before setup work.
- Keep client data private; delete it when asked and confirm in writing.
