# Pitch and outreach: DocuFlow

**Who to message:** accountants and CA firms, e-commerce sellers (supplier bills, courier COD statements), logistics and transport (challans, bilty), distributors and factories (POs, vendor bills), clinics, schools and offices (forms).

**Where to find them:** LinkedIn (search "accounts officer", "finance manager" + city), Google Maps (chartered accountants, distributors), Daraz seller pages, Facebook/Instagram business pages, local trade directories.

## First message (pick one, keep it short)

**A. English**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], I build a tool that turns invoices and forms into Excel automatically. Your team is probably typing these by hand. I can process 20 of your invoices free so you can see the result. Interested?

**B. For accountants / CA firms**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], month-end data entry from client invoices takes hours. I've built a tool that reads PDFs, scans and phone photos of invoices and gives you a checked Excel sheet in minutes (it even catches wrong totals and duplicates). Can I run 20 of your client invoices through it for free?

**C. For e-commerce sellers**
> Hi [Name], saw your store on [Daraz/Instagram]. If you're typing supplier bills or courier statements into Excel, I have a tool that does it automatically from PDFs or photos. Happy to do 20 of yours free, and you just check the result.

**D. Roman Urdu**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], maine aik tool banaya hai jo invoices aur bills (PDF ya photo) se data nikal kar khud Excel bana deta hai. Aap ki team shayad ye sab haath se type karti hai. Main aap ki 20 invoices free mein process kar ke dikha sakta hoon. Dilchaspi hai?

Attach: `sample_output.xlsx` or the 90-second `demo.mp4`.

## Follow-ups
- **After 3-4 days:** "Just checking if you saw this. Here's a 90-second video of it working: [link]. The free test on 20 invoices is still open."
- **After a week:** "Last note from me. If month-end data entry is ever a headache, reply 'test' and send any 20 invoices. I'll return the Excel within a day."

## Common objections

| They say | You answer |
|---|---|
| "Is my data safe?" | Files are private to your account and deleted on the schedule you choose (even right after download). Never used to train AI. Self-hosting available. Show `/privacy`. |
| "How accurate is it?" | Show the measured results and the review screen: every uncertain value is marked orange and a person approves before export, so wrong numbers don't slip through. |
| "Our invoices look different." | That's exactly what the free test is for: 20 of your own invoices. |
| "We already use accounting software." | DocuFlow feeds it: the Excel/CSV matches the import layout you use. |
| "We have handwritten bills." | Handwriting is harder; those get flagged for review. Printed bills and photos work well. |
